About us

Good clothes shouldn't end up in the bin.

ReWeave is a social enterprise that connects people who have clothes to spare with people who need them. Everything we receive is sorted, cleaned and checked for quality.

Wearable items go to individuals, families and community partners. Items that are too worn to wear enter our recycling stream, so nothing is thrown away if it can be reused.

Any income from low-cost sales and recycled materials is reinvested into reaching more people.

Our mission

To give dignified, quality clothing to people in need while keeping textiles out of landfill through reuse and recycling.

Our vision

A community where no one goes without the clothes they need and where every garment is used to the end of its life.

The recycling loop

From donation to second life in four steps

1

Collect

People drop off or schedule pick-ups of clothing they no longer wear.

2

Sort & clean

Volunteers separate wearable items from those that need repair or recycling.

3

Give

Good clothing goes to people in need through partners and community days.

4

Recycle

Worn-out fabric becomes fibre, stuffing or new material instead of waste.

Meet the founder

GABRIEL AKURANG

Founder & Director

ReWeave began with a simple observation: wardrobes full of clothes that were barely worn, and people nearby who could not afford the basics they needed. Gabriel saw that the problem was not a shortage of clothing, but a gap between the people who had it and the people who needed it.

He also saw how much perfectly usable fabric ended up in the bin. So he set out to build a social enterprise that closes both gaps at once, passing good clothes on to people in need and recycling whatever cannot be worn.

Today ReWeave is guided by one idea: every garment deserves more than one life, and every person deserves to feel dignified in what they wear.
